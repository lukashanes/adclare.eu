# Code of Conduct

Adclare contributors are expected to keep discussion professional, respectful and focused on building useful public-interest software.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful of different political, legal and technical contexts.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep issue and pull request discussions on topic.
- Assume good faith, but be precise when something is unsafe or incorrect.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Deliberate disruption of the project.
- Abusive political campaigning in project spaces.

Reports can be sent to `support@adclare.eu`.
